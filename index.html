Non-Negotiables

The Coach's Manifesto

I
No training every day
Mandatory 48-hour muscle recovery between sessions. Breaking this rule guarantees injury and zero results. Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday — that is your schedule.
II
Rest is the workout
The 60–90 second rest between sets is when the adaptation signal is sent to your muscles. Skipping rest doesn't mean training harder — it means training stupidly.
III
Form before reps
Ten perfect reps build a physique. Twenty sloppy reps build an injury. Every exercise must be done through full range of motion before you consider adding volume.
IV
Ditch the shortcut mentality
30-day results are for 30-day programs. This is a 7-month scientific method. The compound interest of consistency is how transformations happen.
V
Protein is non-negotiable
Target 0.8–1g per kg of bodyweight daily — that's 64–80g minimum. Muscle is literally built from dietary protein. No curry replaces this math.
VI
Chin Tucks, every single day
3 sets of 10 chin tucks takes 3 minutes. It corrects Forward Head Posture from desk work and is the #1 contributor to jawline definition alongside fat loss.
